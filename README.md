# Competitive Product Benchmarking & Gap Analysis Dashboard 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Competitive Product Benchmarking & Gap Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI business intelligence project designed to compare a company's product against competitors across feature coverage, feature scores, customer ratings, market share, pricing competitiveness, and feature availability.

The dashboard helps product and strategy teams identify competitive strengths, uncover product gaps, and prioritize areas for improvement based on feature importance and competitor performance.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Benchmark product features against competitors.
- Compare feature coverage across competing products.
- Analyze feature availability and gaps.
- Evaluate weighted feature performance.
- Compare competitor market share.
- Analyze customer ratings.
- Evaluate price competitiveness.
- Identify high-importance feature gaps.
- Support product improvement and competitive strategy decisions.
